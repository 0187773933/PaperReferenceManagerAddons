prma method-imagesprma all-images  ·  Every figure the pipeline cropped , unfiltered -- this page keeps its OWN selection and skip list , separate from /method-images. A purple corner flag ( and the 'In Model Design' pill ) marks a figure you already picked over there ; that's read-only here , so clicking still adds to this page's selection. chips stack : picking several in one row widens it ( fMRI + EEG = either ) , while chips from different rows narrow each other ( fMRI + architecture = architecture figures from fMRI studies ). 'All' clears them. Click anywhere on a figure to add it to your selection ( saved on the server when served , or in this browser off disk -- either way it survives refreshes and re-runs , then Export JSON / CSV ). Keyboard triage : ← / → ( or ↑ / ↓ ) move between papers , Space skips the current one ( skipped papers hide from the list and come back under the 'Skipped' pill ). The bottom-right counter shows which paper you're on out of how many -- type a number there and press Enter to jump straight to it. Solid tags are structural terms ( matched on their own ) ; dashed chips / plain tags are component terms ( + needed per caption ). Pills under a title are the modalities the study used ( from its title , abstract and Methods section ) ; a dashed pill was inferred from the full text because neither an abstract nor a Methods section was available , and may be a modality the paper only discusses. No pills means none was found -- normal for reviews and behavioural work. Figures are built in batches as you scroll : only the papers near the viewport hold real images , the rest hold their place until you reach them ( which is what keeps a 15 , 000-figure report scrollable ). One consequence -- the browser's own find-in-page only sees the papers currently on screen ; everything this page offers ( the chips and their counts , sort , ← / → , the jump box , Export ) still covers the whole report.
